Frequently asked questions

What is MyCal?

MyCal is a calorie and nutrition tracking app for iPhone that logs meals from a photo, your voice or a barcode.

How accurate is photo analysis?

It is an estimate made by an AI model from what is visible on the plate. You see the numbers before saving and can correct them. It is not medical advice.

Is MyCal free?

The app is free to download and use. One photo analysis a day is free; MyCal Plus makes photo analysis unlimited and adds menu scanning and an AI sports coach.

Which languages does it support?

Turkish, English, German, French, Japanese and Korean.

Which foods does the database cover?

MyCal started in Türkiye, so the database is strongest for Turkish dishes and products sold there, and food names from the database appear in English. Photo and voice logging work with any cuisine.

Is my data safe?

Your health data is processed with your explicit consent and stored encrypted on servers in the EU. It is not sold or shared for advertising.

Values are estimates and not medical advice.
